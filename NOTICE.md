Project: FFmpeg  

Website: https://ffmpeg.org/

\## HandBrakeCLI



Project: HandBrake  

Website: https://handbrake.fr/



HandBrakeCLI is used for compression profiles such as CPU x265 and NVIDIA NVENC H.265.

Project: rife-ncnn-vulkan  

Repository: https://github.com/nihui/rife-ncnn-vulkan



rife-ncnn-vulkan is used for RIFE-based frame interpolation.
